| Figure | Surface contact angle | Air temperature | Relative humidity |
|--------|----------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| (a)    | 60° (hydrophilic)        | 22 °C | 45 % |
| (b)    | 60° (hydrophilic)        | 22 °C | 55 % |

(the defrost temperature traces all start from −20.0 °C).
